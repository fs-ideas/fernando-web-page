About

Fernando Silveira is a writer, teacher, entrepreneur, and drummer based in Campinas, Brazil. He writes on the mundane — a dog at a square, a stranger, a small social moment — and finds in it something worth recording. Fernando also writes on philosophy and psychology; to be shared soon. His teaching is built around a conviction that language is best acquired through ideas worth thinking about and tasks worth doing. On the entrepreneurial side, he acts as a developer, product owner and UX thinker at software house with two partners, building web and mobile systems for other companies. Fernando is also developing Forma, a mobile learning app for adults focused on developing how they think, work, relate to others, and handle their emotional lives — using English as the medium of growth rather than the subject of study. He has been playing drums for most of his life, a precious hobby.
